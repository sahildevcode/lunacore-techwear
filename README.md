# LUNACORE™ // SS23 Cybernetic Techwear & Streetwear Store

A production-ready avant-garde techwear e-commerce website with Indian Rupee (₹) pricing, rich multi-section catalog, flash offers, best sellers, client testimonials, and full shopping bag engine.

## 🚀 Live Demo
- **Live Website**: [https://sahildevcode.github.io/lunacore-techwear/](https://sahildevcode.github.io/lunacore-techwear/)
- **Repository**: [https://github.com/sahildevcode/lunacore-techwear](https://github.com/sahildevcode/lunacore-techwear)

## 💎 Features
- **Indian Rupee (₹) Pricing**:
  - All prices formatted with Indian Rupee currency (e.g. `₹4,999`, `₹11,999`) across Catalog, Modals, Cart Drawer, and Checkout.
- **Dedicated Pages & Sections**:
  - **Main Catalog**: 8 high-performance weather-sealed jackets, noragi kimonos, trousers and streetwear apparel.
  - **Best Selling Section (`#best-sellers`)**: Top rated garments (`#1 BESTSELLER`, `4.9/5.0 RATED`, customer volume badges).
  - **Exclusive Offers & Flash Deals (`#offers`)**:
    - Live ticking countdown timer (`HH : MM : SS`).
    - One-click copy coupon codes (`TECH20` - Flat 20% Off, `FESTIVE50` - 50% Flash Off).
    - Prototype flash deals with up to 50% discount tags.
  - **Verified Client Testimonials (`#testimonials`)**:
    - Real customer reviews from Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi NCR, and Hyderabad.
    - Verified purchase badges, star ratings, and techwear durability reports.
  - **Lookbook Volume (`#lookbook`)**: Interactive collection carousel.
  - **Community Grid**: 6 curated streetwear fashion photos.
- **Interactive E-Commerce Suite**:
  - Slide-over shopping bag drawer with quantity counters & subtotal calculations.
  - Quick-view specification modal with size selector (`S`, `M`, `L`, `XL`) and technical fabric specs.
  - Instant live keyword search modal.
  - UPI / GPAY / Card checkout simulation with order invoice generation.
  - Desktop vs Mobile phone frame toggle for client presentation.
